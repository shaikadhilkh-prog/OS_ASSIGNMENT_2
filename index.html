05 · What is happening?

Arrival time

A process joins the ready queue when its arrival time is reached. Processes already waiting stay in queue order.

Time quantum

The CPU runs the queue's first process for at most this many milliseconds, or until it finishes.

Preemption

If work remains when its quantum expires, the process goes to the back of the ready queue so others get CPU time.

Context switch

In Activity 2, changing from one process to another costs 1 ms. This time is shown separately and does not execute a process.
